How to Order a Product

  1. Browse the products and select the one you like.
  2. Click on "Add to Cart" to add the product to your shopping cart.
  3. Proceed to the cart and review your items.
  4. Click "Checkout" and provide your shipping and payment information.
  5. Complete the payment to place your order.

How to Login

  1. Click on the "Sign In" button in the top-right corner of the homepage.
  2. Enter your registered email and password.
  3. Click "Login" to access your account.
  4. If you are new, click on "Sign Up" to create a new account.
